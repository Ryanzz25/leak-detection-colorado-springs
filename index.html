Leak Detection in Colorado Springs: A Hidden Leak Checklist

Most hidden leaks never leave a puddle. Work through this list before you call, and you will be able to describe exactly what you have found.

Did the water bill jump?

Colorado Springs Utilities bills in gallons, so a jump is easy to spot. A pinhole leak can waste thousands of gallons a month with only soft carpet or a musty closet as a clue.

Does the meter move with everything off?

Turn off every fixture and appliance and watch the meter. If it still moves, water is going somewhere.

Can you hear water in the walls?

Running water with all fixtures off points to a supply line inside a wall or under the floor.

Is any floor warm with the heat off?

A warm area on tile usually means a hot water line under the slab; see slab leak detection.

Is there a mold or musty smell in one spot?

A smell that stays in one room or closet is a common sign of a slow, hidden leak.

Is the yard part of the picture?

Irrigation lines feeding xeriscaped yards hide leaks too, and soil movement on hillsides in Manitou Springs and Black Forest can stress buried pipe over the years.
